# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Students, Teachers, and Administrative staff at an educational institution. They need to manage schedules, enrollments, and academic performance efficiently.

## Product Purpose
Midaxus is an academic management platform designed to streamline institutional workflows, providing a central hub for scheduling, course management, and student data visualization.

## Brand Personality
Modern, Minimalist, and Precise. The brand aims to feel like a high-end productivity tool (similar to Linear or Notion) rather than a generic administrative portal.

## Anti-references
- "AI Slop": Generic Tailwind/Bootstrap templates with overused blue/indigo palettes and heavy shadows.
- Nested cards and identical card grids.
- Generic SaaS hero-metric layouts.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity through Structure**: Use hierarchy and scale rather than lines and boxes to separate sections.
- **Intentional Color**: Use a restrained color strategy with tinted neutrals to create a calm, professional environment.
- **Expert Confidence**: The interface should feel like a tool for experts—no hand-holding through cluttered interfaces, but extreme clarity through layout.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Target: WCAG 2.1 AA.
- High focus on readability and clear interactive states.
